# Sentinely listing copy

The same copy is used for the ChatGPT app, the Claude connector and the Codex plugin.

## Name

Sentinely

## Short description

AI social listening and brand protection: mentions, topics, competitors, revenue signals and cases.

## Long description

Sentinely is AI social listening and brand protection. It collects public mentions of the topics you track
across social platforms, forums, app stores and the web, uses AI to judge which ones are relevant to you, and flags
impersonation, scams and other misuse of your brand as cases.

Connect a workspace to bring that work into your assistant. Ask what people are saying about your brand, see sentiment
and share of voice, spot emerging topics, check which platforms are being listened to, see what changed on watched
competitor pages, review revenue signals where buyers ask for alternatives, and read cases with their evidence and
notes. Sentinely also shows the official reporting routes for a platform and can draft a takedown request for you to
review and send yourself.

When you ask it to act, Sentinely can add, tune or pause a topic, label a mention, review a revenue signal, send an
accepted revenue signal to the CRM already connected to your workspace, watch or stop watching a competitor page, open
or annotate a case and move a case to a new status. Topic changes that can raise collection, and every CRM send, first
return a preview and run only after you confirm. Your assistant describes every other change and may ask you to
confirm it first.

Sentinely only returns what it has already collected for the workspace you choose when you connect. It does not browse
sites on demand, never submits takedown reports, and cannot change billing, API keys, members, CRM or account
connections, or delete a workspace. Text captured from public sources is treated as untrusted data.

## Starter prompts

1. What are people saying about my brand this week?
2. Show share of voice and sentiment across my tracked topics.
3. What changed on my competitors' pages recently?
4. Show my open impersonation cases with severity 80 or higher.
